STATNUSA — an independent presentation of Indonesia's public statistics. Figures come from BPS (Badan Pusat Statistik) and APJII, cited on every page. This site is not affiliated with BPS or any government body.

All data on this site is free to read and download. No account, no tracking, no network request: the site runs entirely from its own files.
